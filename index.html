Mixing AI Into Live Edits

On Atelier Madre's Reels, generative elements are composited directly into live-action footage during the edit itself, not swapped in as a separate asset. AI generated textures, color washes and motion overlays are timed to the cut, graded to match the brand's footage, and blended in DaVinci Resolve and After Effects so the AI layer reads as part of the shot, not on top of it. The same daily publishing pace applies here as with any other Reel: quick turnaround, consistent look, no extra production day. AI generated B-roll and textures also let me rapidly test multiple visual variations of the same edit without needing a new shoot day — useful for A/B testing hooks and openers at speed.

ToolsWeavy AI, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve
